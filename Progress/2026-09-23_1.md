## Decisions and tradeoffs

- Kept Slice 1 observational only. Temporary track IDs, geometry, and helmet
  metrics do not participate in alert timing, classification, recovery, or
  cooldown decisions.
- Used a small internal mutable dataclass for state carried across frames and
  immutable dataclasses for observations intended for serialization.
- Used deterministic greedy IoU matching instead of a full identity tracker.
  This is bounded and appropriate for diagnostic evidence, but it can still
  switch identities when people cross or heavily overlap.
- Treated the numeric limits as initial resource guardrails, not thresholds
  derived from production false-positive data. They should be revisited after
  diagnostic truncation, occupancy, and track-fragmentation data is collected.
- Kept file persistence out of `safety_alerts.py`. The runtime runner should
  drain and persist observations in the next implementation slice.
